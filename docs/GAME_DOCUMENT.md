## 1. Game name
**BUILDVERSE — Find Words, Build Your World.** A web game in a blocky pixel style. The player finds English words, places things in a room, and earns coins to decorate a house.

## 2. Target learners
Lower-secondary students (Grade 7–9) and adult beginners, **CEFR A1–A2**. All screens and instructions are in English. Thai help can be turned on or off.

## 3. Language goals
After playing, learners can:
- **Vocabulary:** recognise and spell **75 words for things in the home** (15 per room: bedroom, living room, kitchen, bathroom, garden).
- **Prepositions of place:** understand and use **on, under, next to, between** to say where things are.
- **Reading:** read short English clues (*"You boil water in this."*) and follow instructions (*"Put the rug under the table."*).

## 4. How to play
1. Press **Play** and choose an open room. Only the Bedroom is open at the start.
2. **Find Words:** read the clue, look at the picture, and drag across the letters in the grid to find the word. Each found word shows its meaning, an example sentence and its sound. Hints are free.
3. **Place Items:** read the English instruction and drag the item to the correct place. Press **Check Placement**. If it is wrong, the game explains why and gives a hint.
4. **Rewards:** get coins. Then play again, choose another room, or decorate **My House** with furniture from the **Shop**.

| Level | Words | Grid | Word directions | Instructions | Coins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 5 | 8×8 | → ↓ | 1 | 50 |
| Normal | 7 | 10×10 | + diagonal | 2 | 70 |
| Hard | 9 | 12×12 | + backwards | 3 | 100 |

Bonuses: **+20** for finishing in time (optional Challenge Mode), **+100** the first time a room is cleared.

## 5. Core loop
**Choose a room → Find Words → Place Items → Rewards → repeat**

Clearing a room opens the next one. Every round picks new words, a new grid and new instructions, so students can replay any room. Coins let players decorate their own house, which keeps them playing and practising.
